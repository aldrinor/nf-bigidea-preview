Ultrafine particles don't spread out evenly

A global model at 1-kilometer resolution mapped ultrafine particles across North America. Over open land the count sits near 700 particles in a cubic centimeter of air. Along the highways and over the cities it climbs to several thousand, and past 36,000 at the worst spots. The map traces nearly every interstate and every city.
