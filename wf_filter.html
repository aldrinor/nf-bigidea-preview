Ordinary filters

A charge that fades under smoke

~95%

of its cleaning power — gone in a few hours.

Pine smoke carries an oily, organic load that strips an electret filter's built-in charge. In an EPA study, a charged filter lost about 95% of its cleaning power within hours of smoke — while the airflow barely changed, so nothing warns you it has stopped working.

Source — Holder et al., EPA, Indoor Air, 2022 ↗
