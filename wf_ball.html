Wildfire Smoke is spreading and deadly —and it's electrically charged

Wildfire smoke reaches nearly every American every year, and kills about 41,000 of them — projected to reach 71,000 by 2050. It's mostly ultrafine particles, and they carry an electric charge. A study by the EPA found the filter the U.S. government recommends loses 95% of its cleaning power after just 10 milligrams of smoke.
