Every speck of smokecarries a charge

Wildfire smoke is mostly soot. The particles are minute — the freshest are only about 30 to 50 nanometres across, and most stay under 1001 — small enough to ride deep into the lungs. And almost every one of them carries an electric charge2.

Sources
1.Particle size — Hosseini et al., Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics, 2010
2.Carries an electric charge — Suhonen et al., Journal of Cleaner Production, 2021
